A few words before we begin

Islam made marriage simple and light, easy, and without hardship. Over time our customs carried it far from that simplicity, and made it difficult and expensive.

The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said that marriage completes half of one's faith. The other half, as I understand it, is that we keep learning and growing better together.

This journey is about building something from nothing, facing hardship side by side, and finding joy in small victories, not about an exchange of goods.

The world is changing, and we should think again about our customs, so that weddings stay simple and full of joy.

May Allah make it easy for everyone. Ameen.
